## Joint workflows (proposals, marked as such)
- **W1. A behaviour policy becomes an executable constitution.** RiskMandate writes an agent's ABP. Its rules become predicates in TauGuard's governance core. Every consequence-bearing call goes through the core: APPROVE, REVIEW or BLOCK. Each decision goes to the Chronicle and back into the agent's run report.
- **W2. The Sovereign Website change chain under ARCHONT.** Our R4 chain runs: brief, sites review, Factory request, security, decision, merge. It is already authority as commit, with signed evidence. ARCHONT would hold it as governed state: who may decide; Standing; and a change's impact through dependencies (a repository, its keys, its domain). TauStamp would be the lineage. Our provenance graph is the evidence feed.
- **W3. PrompTugal News: governed facts before release.** The newsroom has a desks' pipeline, a gate (validate.js) and a runs ledger. TauDIL with SYGON checks each article's claims for semantic coherence against its sources before the gate passes. When meaning cannot be established, the article stops (refusal is terminal). Drift between releases is reported.
- **W4. TauGuard's own agents under a RiskMandate policy.** RiskMandate writes ABPs for the agents TauGuard runs or demonstrates (for example, the TauDIL demo). TauGuard's core enforces them. This is RiskMandate's "policy, hook, boundary" ladder with TauGuard as the boundary.

## The MVP that works both ways
- **Our side:** one of our agents (proposed: a site keeper's push to dev) sends each consequence-bearing action to a TauGuard decision endpoint before acting. It acts only on APPROVE. The decision lands in the run report and on the site's /agents/ provenance.
- **Their side:** one PrompTugal News article runs through TauDIL/SYGON before release, with the result shown next to our gate's result.
- **Measure:** decisions logged, blocks that were right, false blocks, latency added. Published on the site, as RiskMandate publishes its own counts.
- **Needs from TauGuard:** an endpoint or a demo build, and their word on what can be shown. None is assumed here.
